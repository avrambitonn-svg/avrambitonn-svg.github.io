before you get in the water

Briefing

Do I need to be a strong swimmer?

You need to feel comfortable in the water. On an intro dive your instructor stays beside you the whole time, and if anything feels wrong, you go up.

Do I need a medical certificate?

Before every dive you fill in a health declaration. If you have a medical condition, such as heart or lung problems, asthma or ear problems, talk to us first. In some cases a doctor’s approval is required.

Can I fly after diving?

Under the common DAN guidelines, wait at least 12 hours after a single dive and at least 18 hours after several dives or several days of diving. Plan your dive early in your holiday.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, a towel and drinking water. If you use sunscreen, choose a reef-safe one. We bring the diving equipment.

What is the minimum age?

Intro dives from age 8. Each course has its own minimum age under its standards; ask us and we’ll check.

Do you dive in winter?

Yes. Eilat’s water stays fairly warm all year, about 21° in winter and 27° in summer.

When are you open?

By appointment. Call or message us on WhatsApp and we’ll find a time that suits you.
